# Name, logo and characters

The code here is free for anyone to use, change and sell, under the licence in [LICENSE](LICENSE). That licence covers the code only.

The names **Oddly Clever**, **Oddly Clever Tools**, **OddlyCleverEditor**, **OddlyClever Design** and **Clearpane**, the logo, and the Oddly Clever characters (Sparks and the rest of the crew) are not part of that licence. They stay with Oddly Clever Tools.

What that means in practice:

- **Fine:** using the tools for any project, paid client work included; forking the code and building your own product; saying your product is "based on OddlyCleverEditor" or "built on Clearpane".
- **Please don't:** releasing a fork under our name or logo; using the characters in your own branding; doing anything that makes people think your version comes from us.

If you fork it, give it a new name and a new look. Not sure if something's OK? Open an issue and ask.
